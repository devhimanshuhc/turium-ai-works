# Knowledge Inbox product FAQ

Knowledge Inbox lets a single user save notes, links and files into knowledge bases and ask questions over them.

Supported file types are PDF, DOCX, TXT and Markdown, up to 10 MB each. Links are fetched on the server, and pages pointing to private networks are refused.

Each chat is locked to the knowledge bases chosen when it was created; they cannot be changed later. Answers cite numbered sources, and a groundedness check marks each answer as grounded, partially grounded or ungrounded.
