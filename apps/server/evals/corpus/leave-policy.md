# Leave policy (India)

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days carry over to the next year; anything above 10 days lapses on 31 December.

Sick leave is separate: 12 days per year, with no carry-over. A doctor's certificate is required for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days.

Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver. Leave requests are submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance, except for sick leave.
